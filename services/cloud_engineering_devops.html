Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cloud services

How long does a typical cloud migration take?

Migration timelines vary based on complexity, but most projects complete within 8-16 weeks. We work in phases to minimize disruption and ensure zero downtime.

What cloud platforms do you support?

We're platform-agnostic and work with AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and hybrid/multi-cloud environments. Our expertise spans all major cloud providers.

How do you ensure security and compliance?

We implement industry-standard security practices including encryption, IAM policies, network segmentation, and compliance frameworks (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, etc.).

What kind of ongoing support do you provide?

We offer 24/7 monitoring, incident response, regular optimization reviews, and dedicated DevOps engineers. Support levels are customizable based on your needs.

Can you help reduce our current cloud costs?

Absolutely. Our AI-powered cost optimization typically reduces cloud spending by 30-50% through right-sizing, reserved instances, and eliminating waste.
