Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about system integration and migration

How long does a typical migration take?

Most migrations complete within 4-8 weeks, depending on system complexity, data volume, and customization requirements. We provide a detailed timeline after the initial assessment.

Will there be any downtime during migration?

Our phased migration approach ensures zero downtime for business-critical systems. We use parallel running and staged cutover strategies to maintain continuous operations.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Every migration includes comprehensive rollback procedures and backup strategies. Our team monitors the process 24/7 and can revert to the previous state if needed.

Do you provide post-migration support?

Yes, we offer 90 days of post-migration support included, with options for extended support packages. Our team remains available to address any issues and optimize performance.

Can you migrate custom or legacy systems?

Absolutely. We specialize in complex legacy system migrations and can handle custom-built applications, proprietary databases, and unique integration requirements.
