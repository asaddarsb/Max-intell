Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files that are placed on your computer, mobile device, or other device when you visit our website. They contain information about your browsing activity and preferences and are used to recognize you when you return to our site.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled. They enable basic functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas.

Analytical Cookies: These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They collect data about website usage patterns to help us improve our services.

Preference Cookies: These cookies remember your preferences and choices to provide a personalized experience on subsequent visits.

Marketing Cookies: These cookies track your activity across websites to deliver targeted advertising and measure marketing effectiveness.

3. How We Use Cookies

  • To recognize you when you return to our website
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To understand how you use our website
  • To improve our website functionality and user experience
  • To deliver personalized content and advertisements
  • To analyze website traffic and user behavior

4. Cookie Lifespan

Some cookies are "session cookies" which expire when you close your browser. Others are "persistent cookies" which remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control how cookies are used through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are set and remove specific cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Accept or reject cookies when visiting new websites

Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may include content from third parties that set their own cookies. We are not responsible for the cookies set by these third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy and cookie policies.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us.
